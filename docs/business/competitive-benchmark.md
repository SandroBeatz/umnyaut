# Competitive Benchmark

> Version 1.0 · 2026-10-09 · [Business](../business/)

## Overview

**Qalculator.ru is UmnyAut's primary benchmark competitor.** It is the closest public product to the original concept: renovation calculators with results in purchase units, waste allowance, related materials, price, explanation, shareable links, print/PDF, standards references and no required registration.

This changes the competitive baseline. Packs, related materials, a saved link and a methodology block are no longer a defensible headline advantage by themselves. UmnyAut's position is therefore:

> **«Введите размеры один раз — получите проверенный список покупок для всего ремонта».**

The homepage H1 and short version are: **«Одна комната — весь список покупок».**

UmnyAut should not race Qalculator on page count. It should win on a connected room workflow, calculation depth, independently verified formulas and one merged list across several works.

## Benchmark snapshot

Checked on 2026-10-09. Public observations can change and must be rechecked before product or SEO decisions.

| Dimension | Qalculator | Implication for UmnyAut |
|---|---|---|
| Breadth | 27 categories; 143 calculator entries counted on the home page; 145 URLs in the sitemap | Do not use raw tool count as a launch KPI |
| Core result | Purchase units, waste, price and related materials on major tools | These are category requirements, not positioning |
| Actions | URL sharing, copy, print/PDF; no mandatory account | UmnyAut must reach parity, then differentiate through the connected project |
| Trust | Standards/methodology references and explanatory text | Source links alone are parity; dated sources, golden cases and reviewer sign-off form the stronger claim |
| Distribution | Search-oriented catalog, embeddable widget, custom-calculator offer | Widget is not a unique future advantage; it needs configurability or catalog integration to be paid-worthy |
| Connected workflow | Individual calculators and a separate estimate tool; no cross-tool shared room or automatically merged room list was found in the public UX/code inspected | This is UmnyAut's primary product gap to own |
| Formula depth | Some pages expose many options, but the inspected laminate implementation reduces material need to area × coefficients ÷ pack area; displayed row counts do not model offset/offcut reuse | Build fewer tools with real geometry and explain material differences |
| Growth signal | A young site has already assembled a broad, fast catalog | Demand and distribution are plausible, but speed of imitation is a material risk |

The owner-provided Similarweb snapshot for September 2026 estimated 11.3k visits, 93.1% bounce, 1 page per visit, 31.3% organic, 36.4% direct and 1.31% AI traffic. This is directional third-party data, not a factual KPI baseline. The combination of some traffic and very shallow sessions is consistent with a useful single-answer SEO tool, but it does not prove retention or a connected-project use case.

## Product decision

### What is now parity

- purchase units and rounding to whole packs, rolls, bags or cans;
- waste allowance, openings and related-material prompts;
- optional price and total;
- explanation and source links;
- shareable URL, print/PDF and no-registration use;
- mobile performance and indexable calculator pages.

Shipping these well is mandatory, but they must not be presented as the sole reason to choose UmnyAut.

### Where UmnyAut must be better

1. **One input, many works.** Room dimensions and openings are entered once and reused without retyping.
2. **A connected renovation chain.** For example: room → wall area → wallpaper → glue → primer → one merged shopping list.
3. **Calculation depth where geometry matters.** Laminate rows, offsets, narrow last row and reusable offcuts; wallpaper strips and repeat; tile grid and cuts. A fixed waste coefficient is only a fallback.
4. **Auditable accuracy.** Primary sources, checked dates, at least ten golden examples, invariants and paid review by a practising finisher. A competitor result is a comparison point, never a source of truth.
5. **Decision-ready result.** The first screen answers what to buy, what remains, what else is needed and what to calculate next.

### Launch sequencing

Breadth is subordinate to proving the signature workflow. After the two data-donor tools, the first complete path should be the wall cluster: **room → wall area → wallpaper/paint → glue or primer → merged list**. The floor cluster follows with laminate → underlay → plinth. An unrelated calculator must not displace a missing link in the active chain merely to increase the catalog count.

The 30-tool research list remains useful as a ranked backlog. The 60–80 figure is a conditional ceiling for validated clusters, not a success metric or a promise.

## Mandatory benchmark protocol

For every calculator that overlaps Qalculator, research and release review include at least three shared scenarios:

1. a typical room and material;
2. a boundary case that exposes rounding, repeat, cuts or a narrow remainder;
3. a case with an opening, non-default layout or non-default pack.

Record:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Inputs | Same units and assumptions on both products |
| Qalculator output | Result, visible assumptions and observation date |
| UmnyAut output | Result, warnings, steps and related items |
| Independent expected result | Manual derivation or primary manufacturer/standard source |
| Difference | Exact numeric delta and the assumption that explains it |
| Product gap | UX, connected-flow or trust advantage still missing |
| Content gap | Query or explanation the page must answer |

A Qalculator value may reveal a defect or useful edge case. It must never be copied into a golden example without independent derivation. If UmnyAut differs, the product should explain why instead of silently forcing parity.

## Review cadence

- Recheck the home page, sitemap, overlapping wave tools and monetization before each release wave.
- Recheck formula behaviour for each overlapping tool during formula review.
- Review the benchmark quarterly after launch: new calculators, traffic direction, new connected features and changes to sharing/widget offers.
- Track competitive findings in the research table, not in production code.
