# Wordstat Snapshot, October 2026

> Version 1.0 · 2026-10-20 · [SEO and Analytics](./seo-and-analytics.md)

## Overview

Real Yandex Wordstat demand for the 10 live tools, taken to rewrite titles, descriptions, H1/`question` and FAQ in `apps/web/content/tools/*.md`. Source: wordstat.yandex.ru, «Топы запросов», all regions, all devices, period **08.09.2026 – 07.10.2026** (a low-season month; spring numbers will be higher). Counts are broad-match monthly impressions (each line includes its longer variants).

Filtered out: prices («сколько стоит», «цена»), weight, labour cost, shops and cities, brands' own calculators, unrelated meanings (hair dye, car paint, paving tiles, chocolate bars, the film «Похороните меня за плинтусом», school geometry). These are not our intent and must not drive copy.

Queries are quoted as Wordstat returns them (lower case, no punctuation). Use them as phrasing, not as text to paste.

## Signals for the snippets

1. **«на комнату» is the main refinement** for wallpaper, laminate and linoleum — keep it in titles.
2. **Pack/roll language wins**: «сколько рулонов», «сколько пачек», «сколько в упаковке» are bigger than any «калькулятор» phrase. Our answer is literally in packs and rolls; titles should say so.
3. **Layout schemes have demand**: «со схемой (укладки)», «раскладка» for laminate (≈ 1 800 combined) and tile (≈ 900). Phase 16 schemes are a selling point for titles/descriptions.
4. **Wallpaper: «с учетом дверей» (≈ 450) and «рапорт»** — we handle both; say so.
5. **Tile: «для ванной» (≈ 3 000+) and «на стену»** are as big as «на пол»; the tool does both.
6. **Grout: epoxy is ≈ 1/4 of demand**; tile size examples («600х600», «300х300») recur.
7. **Adhesive: «на 1м2» dominates**; trowel/layer thickness and porcelain stoneware are the refinements.
8. **Area tools are mostly «как посчитать»** — how-to intent: formula + example first, calculator second; «площадь стен по площади пола» is a frequent wrong shortcut worth an FAQ.
9. **Number-led FAQ**: «на 18 квадратных метров», «комната 3 на 4», «4 на 4» recur across tools — one worked-example FAQ per tool.
